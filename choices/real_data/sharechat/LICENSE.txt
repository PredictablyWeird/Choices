Dataset: ShareChat - A Dataset of Chatbot Conversations in the Wild
Source: https://huggingface.co/datasets/tucnguyen/ShareChat

From the paper:
    Yan et al., "ShareChat: A Dataset of Chatbot Conversations in the Wild"
    arXiv:2512.17843

License: Gated access on HuggingFace. Data collected from publicly shared
conversation URLs on ChatGPT, Perplexity, Grok, Gemini, and Claude.

production models without further filtering.
