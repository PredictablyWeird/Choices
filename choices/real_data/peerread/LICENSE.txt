From the NAACL 2018 paper:
    Kang et al., "A Dataset of Peer Reviews (PeerRead): Collection, Insights
    and NLP Applications"

The dataset aggregates papers and reviews from ACL, NeurIPS, and ICLR
(via OpenReview). Individual venues may have their own terms of use.
The HuggingFace page lists the license as "Unknown".
