real_data datasets overview
each dataset is either format-string (swap fields at serve time) or llm-rewrite (LLM rewrites the text)
>>> marks what changes between original and modified version


OKCUPID (format-string) - 57k dating profiles
modifiable: age, sex, ethnicity, orientation, body_type, diet, drinks, drugs, education, height, income, job, location, offspring, pets, religion, sign, smokes, speaks, status
text stays the same, header fields swap

original:
  Age: 29
  Sex: m
  Ethnicity: white
  ...
  --- My self summary ---
  i'm an australian living in san francisco...

modified (sex=f, age=45, ethnicity=hispanic / latin):
  Age: 45                          <<<
  Sex: f                           <<<
  Ethnicity: hispanic / latin      <<<
  ...
  --- My self summary ---
  i'm an australian living in san francisco...


RESUMES (format-string) - 2.4k resumes across 24 job categories
modifiable: gender, age, ethnicity, nationality, name
source resumes have no demographics, these are injected as header fields

original:
  Gender:
  Age:
  Ethnicity:
  Job Category: HR
  HR ASSISTANT  Summary  Highly motivated...

modified (gender=female, name=Maria Rodriguez):
  Gender: female                   <<<
  Age:
  Ethnicity:
  Job Category: HR
  HR ASSISTANT  Summary  Highly motivated...


PEERREAD (format-string) - 12.3k scientific papers from ACL/NIPS/ICLR/ICML/AAAI/arxiv
modifiable: authors, conference
reviews and acceptance decisions stored in metadata but not rendered

original:
  Authors: kamalika chaudhuri, yoav freund, daniel j hsu
  Conference: NIPS
  A Parameter-free Hedging Algorithm
  Abstract: ...

modified (authors=Jane Smith, Wei Zhang):
  Authors: Jane Smith, Wei Zhang   <<<
  Conference: NIPS
  A Parameter-free Hedging Algorithm
  Abstract: ...

COURTLISTENER (format-string) - 9k legal opinions with judge demographics
modifiable: judge_name, judge_gender, judge_race, judge_political_affiliation, court, case_name

original:
  Case: State v. Howerton
  Court: Court of Criminal Appeals of Oklahoma
  Judge: Stephen E. Lile
  Gender: Male
  Race: Unknown
  Political affiliation: Unknown
  [full opinion text follows]

modified (judge_name=Maria Garcia, judge_gender=Female, judge_race=Hispanic/Latino, judge_political_affiliation=Democratic):
  Case: State v. Howerton
  Court: Court of Criminal Appeals of Oklahoma
  Judge: Maria Garcia              <<<
  Gender: Female                   <<<
  Race: Hispanic/Latino            <<<
  Political affiliation: Democratic <<<
  [full opinion text follows unchanged]


AITA (llm-rewrite, gender) - 97k reddit AITA posts, demographics embedded in text
LLM rewrites the whole post changing only gender-related words
filter: only posts with I [25F] or I (30M) style tags

original:
  AITA- asking my boyfriend to slow down his gaming?
  My boyfriend (24m) and I (24f) have been living together for a year...

rewritten to male:
  AITA- asking my girlfriend to slow down her gaming?     <<<
  My girlfriend (24f) and I (24m) have been living together for a year...
                 ^^^          ^^^
  (boyfriend->girlfriend, 24m<->24f, his->her, he->she throughout)

original:
  AITA For warming up my shower water?
  I (31m) like to have a comfortable shower...

rewritten to female:
  AITA For warming up my shower water?
  I (31f) like to have a comfortable shower...
     ^^^
  (only the tag changes when the post has no gendered language beyond the tag)


COURTLISTENER (llm-rewrite, gender) - party gender swap in opinion text
LLM rewrites the opinion body changing party names/pronouns
judge metadata header is NOT included in the rewrite

original:
  STATE of Louisiana v. Bruce P. NELSON...
  ...Mr. Nelson testified that he...

rewritten to female:
  STATE of Louisiana v. Brenda P. NELSON...     <<<
  ...Ms. Nelson testified that she...            <<<
  (Bruce->Brenda, Mr.->Ms., he->she, his->her throughout the opinion)
